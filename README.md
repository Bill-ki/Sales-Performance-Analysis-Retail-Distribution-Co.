# Sales-Performance-Analysis-Retail-Distribution-Co.

## Project Overview
This project analyses sales performance for a retail and distribution business, with a focus on revenue generation, order trends, product performance, customer contribution, returns, cancellations, and fulfilment.

## Analysis Performed
- Evaluated revenue, order volume, units sold, and average order value.
- Analysed sales performance by product category, customer, region, and sales representative.
- Examined order cancellations, returns, and discount patterns.
- Assessed fulfilment performance and identified areas requiring operational attention.

## Key Findings
- Sporting Goods, Apparel, and Office Supplies are the leading categories by revenue.
- Approximately 30% of orders are cancelled or returned.
- The top five customers contribute approximately 18.6% of total revenue.
- The analysis highlights opportunities to improve order outcomes and customer retention.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Excel Formulas & Functions
- Excel Charts & Dashboards
- PostgreSQL

## Business Recommendations
Improve monitoring of cancellations and returns, evaluate discount effectiveness, and use customer and product performance insights to support sales planning.

## Data Note
The analysis uses a synthetic practice dataset and is intended for analytical demonstration purposes.
